Centamont · For developers

For developers, from the first sketch.

We take on few projects, and a founder stays on every one of them. Here is how a building comes to market with us beside it, and how to begin.

Before launch

Pricing, unit mix, floor plans, amenities, buyer profile and launch strategy are settled with the developer before a single residence is offered in public.

Then the plan behind a building’s sales: the gallery, campaigns, a dedicated project website and the team that runs the launch, under one set of rules and one stream of data.

An engagement in six steps

  1. 01We arrive while the building is still a sketch. Advisory begins before the design is final, so the building is shaped around the buyer who will actually live there.
  2. 02A strategy written for this building alone. No template. Positioning, buyer targets and campaigns are designed around your project. A founder sits on your account from the first meeting to the last closing, with no hand-off to juniors after signing.
  3. 03Launch day, with everything already running. The project website and search, the journal and market updates, social media and the business press: all of it is live before the first reservation. A small, senior sales team works the launch, each member owning a buyer segment, under one set of rules and one stream of data.
  4. 04The numbers, every week. Inquiries, tours, reservations, contracts and pricing moves arrive in one weekly report. You always know where the building stands and what we are changing next.
  5. 05Topped off, and still a step ahead. As the structure rises, pricing and releases move with demand, so the last floors are priced as carefully as the first.
  6. 06The last closing is not the last of us. We stay in touch with every buyer who would like us to, so the next building begins with people who already know us.

Measured every week

Every Monday you receive the same one-sheet report: inquiries, tours, reservations, contracts and pricing moves, measured against the plan, with what we are changing next.

How an engagement begins

  1. 01A first conversation with the partners. Tell us about the site, the timeline and where design stands.
  2. 02A study of the project. Financing, approvals, design team and track record. We take on few buildings, and only ones we believe in.
  3. 03A written proposal for your building. The strategy, the team and the milestones we will report against.

Every engagement is reported against its plan, every week. Case studies appear here only with each developer’s written approval.
