Privacy & accessibility

What this site collects

Nothing. This site has no forms, no accounts, no analytics and no advertising cookies. We do not sell or share personal information.

To display our typefaces, your browser loads fonts from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address as part of that request. Google's own privacy policy applies to it.

When you write to us on Instagram, LinkedIn or X, that platform's privacy policy covers the message. We use what you send only to reply and to work with you, and we keep it private.

If we add analytics, a contact form or email in the future, this page will be updated first.

Accessibility

We aim to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 at level AA: readable contrast in light and dark modes, full keyboard access, visible focus, and respect for reduced-motion settings. If anything on this site is hard to use, write to @centamont and a partner will help directly.

Fair housing

Centamont supports the Fair Housing Act and equal housing opportunity. We do not discriminate on the basis of race, color, religion, sex, disability, familial status or national origin, or any other class protected by federal, Florida or local law.
