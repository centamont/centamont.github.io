Why it fits

  • The only three-bedroom line facing the bay at this price.
  • A split plan: the main bedroom on its own side of the living room.
  • The lender’s pre-sale threshold is met and construction is under way, so the building is financed.

What to weigh

Ready about six months after the date you named, and near the top of your range.
