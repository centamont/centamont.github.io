# Centamont website

Static site for centamont.com: a hand-written `index.html` (a single scroll page with anchored sections; the design and product notes, DESIGN.md and PRODUCT.md, are kept in the project folder at `/mnt/project-files/website/notes/`, not in this public repo: read them there, copy them to the repo root if a design skill needs them, and never commit them; `.gitignore` keeps them out), shared styles written and commented in `_src/site.css`, and the styles only one template uses in its own sheet beside it: `_src/home.css` (the three home pages), `_src/practice.css` (the home pages, developers, private clients, advisors, the markets hub, and their es/pt twins), `_src/market.css` (the market pages), `_src/reading.css` (the journal, the es/pt buyer guides, privacy, terms, 404) and `_src/sheet.css` (report, planner, short list, project site) (`tools/version.py` builds the comment-free copies pages load, and Jekyll never publishes `_src/`; never edit the built files by hand; a rule goes in a template sheet only if no page outside that template has its elements, since other pages don't load it, and it must not tie with a later rule in `site.css` on the same element, since it now loads after it; when in doubt, keep it in `site.css`), shared header behavior (menu, day/night, drawings that draw on arrival) written and commented in `_src/site.js`, the tower model in `_src/model.js` and its WebGL renderer in `_src/tower3d.js` (the hero is the live drawing on every screen, after a blind panel preferred it; the rendered stills in `hero/`, listed in `STILLS` in `model.js`, now close the home's developer story at the last closing as plates, `hero/plate-*.webp`, picked by the sun: after the stills change, run `node tools/plates.mjs`, then `python3 tools/rights.py`), the sales-curve instrument and letter composer in `_src/instrument.js`, the launch planner (`planner.html`) in `_src/planner.js`, the home page's behavior in `_src/home.js` (`tools/version.py` writes the published `site.js`, `instrument.js`, `planner.js`, `home.js`, `model.js` and `tower3d.js` from these six sources without their comments and blank lines; edit the sources, never the built files), the developer page `developers.html` and the markets hub `markets.html`, the specimen short list in `shortlist.html`, the specimen project site in `project-site.html` (its demonstration register form, which sends nothing, in `project-site.js`, which answers in the page's language), the dated market sheets on the open market pages written by `tools/marketsheet.py` (edit its table, then run it; a quarterly scheduled task drafts the refresh PR), the markets hub's rows (each market's photograph crop and date, read from its page) written by `tools/hub.py` (rerun it when a market page's opener or date changes; a test checks the dates), the one natural grade every city photograph and film on the market pages carries in `tools/grade.py` (a new photograph or film goes through it, so the set stays one evening), the vellum texture in `grain.png`, journal notes in `journal/`, the market pages `south-florida.html` and `new-york.html`, Spanish and Portuguese versions of every page in `es/` and `pt/` (the home, private clients, developers, markets hub and market pages, every journal note and the journal's index, the launch planner, the weekly report, the short list, the project site, the advisors page, privacy and terms) (root-absolute links; when their English page changes, carry the change over), plus `privacy.html`, `terms.html` (linked from every footer's legal line) and `404.html`, served by GitHub Pages from `main`. Typefaces are self-hosted in `fonts/` (latin woff2 from @fontsource 5.3.0, OFL) and declared at the top of `_src/site.css`; don't add Google Fonts links back. `email/signature-lockup.png` (the logo in Dylan's Gmail signature, which every sent email loads from this address: never move, rename or redraw it in place; a new version takes a new file name), `CNAME`, `robots.txt`, `sitemap.xml`, `og.png`, `apple-touch-icon.png` and the web app manifest `site.webmanifest` (with its `icon-192.png` and `icon-512.png`, the header mark on ink; every page links it from its `<head>`, and new pages copy the link) sit beside it. Subpages copy the header and footer from `index.html`; when one changes, update all pages and add new pages to `sitemap.xml`.

- Every page ends its `<head>` with the same `<script type="speculationrules">` block, which prefetches (never prerenders, so the tower never runs unseen) the page a visitor hovers or presses; new pages copy it, and a Playwright test checks every page.
- Every page's footer (the title block: sheet, drawn date, revision) is written by `python3 tools/footer.py` from its `PAGES` table; revisions live in `tools/footer.json`. After changing any page, run it (with `tools/version.py` and `tools/csp.py`); a new page first gets a row in `PAGES`. Never edit a footer by hand: a test runs `tools/footer.py --check`. The signature above the block is `media/sig.svg`, stamped with the shared `?v=` token.
- Every page is checked by a Playwright test against WCAG 2.2 AA with axe (`@axe-core/playwright`; desktop by day and night, phone by day, motion reduced) and for text cut off by a box with hidden overflow (on a phone and at 320 wide); a label that runs past a drawing's edge fails it, a label wholly outside on purpose passes. `tools/speed.mjs` (the "Phone speed" workflow, on pull requests and weekly, not required) times the main pages with Lighthouse's phone and holds each to a blocking-time budget and full accessibility, best-practice and search marks.
- Every page has a hash-based Content-Security-Policy meta tag. After editing any inline `<script>`, run `python3 tools/csp.py`; the "security policy" Playwright test fails if a hash is stale.
- The home pages' three inline scripts (`index.html`, `es/index.html`, `pt/index.html`) are written and commented in `_src/inline/` (`home-head.js`, `home-intro.js`, `home-hero.js`); the rest of the home's behavior is `_src/home.js`, built to the external `home.js` so the three pages share one cached copy (one source for all three languages, which picks its words and time format from `<html lang>`). `tools/version.py` writes each `<script data-source="_src/inline/NAME.js">` body without comments: edit the source, never the page's copy, then run `python3 tools/version.py` and `python3 tools/csp.py`. A new commented inline script goes the same way; a test fails on a published inline script that carries comments.
- The markets maps (`index.html`, `markets.html` and their es/pt twins) place their plain layers (state lines, borders, the world inset, the survey's coast and islands) from `media/maps.svg` with `<use href="/media/maps.svg?v=…#id">`, so they are fetched once and cached. Every `<use>` inside a `<mask>` or `<clipPath>` points at geometry in the page's own `<defs>` (the land, `#ovLand`, `#ovIsle`, `#svLandP`, `#svSolid`), never at the file, since Safari is unreliable there. The labels, routes and points stay in each page, and the six stay identical. Properties that don't inherit through `<use>` (`vector-effect`, `clip-rule`) sit on the paths in `maps.svg`; everything else is styled on the `<use>`. `tools/version.py` stamps it with the shared `?v=` token.
- Every page links `site.css` and `site.js`, then its template's sheet (`practice.css` and `home.css` on the three home pages, in that order; `practice.css`, `market.css`, `reading.css` or `sheet.css` elsewhere; a Playwright test checks each page's sheets, so a new page is placed in its template there); a page links the other scripts only if it uses them (`model.js` on the three home pages and `404.html`, `tower3d.js` on the three home pages, `instrument.js` on `index.html`, `private-clients.html`, `developers.html`, `advisors.html` and their es/pt twins, `home.js` on the three home pages, `planner.js` on `planner.html`, `project-site.js` on `project-site.html`). All share one `?v=` token. After editing `project-site.js` or any source in `_src/` (the six stylesheets, `site.js`, `instrument.js`, `planner.js`, `home.js`, `model.js`, `tower3d.js`), run `python3 tools/version.py`, then `python3 tools/csp.py`; the "assets carry the current version" test fails on a stale token, and `python3 tools/version.py --check` (run by a test) names a built file that is stale.
- Visits are counted cookie-free by GoatCounter (code `centamont`) from `site.js`, only on centamont.com and never under a test driver or with GPC/DNT; `window.cmCount(name)` counts an event (letters, planner, Instagram, brief). `tools/csp.py` allows that one host; `privacy.html` describes exactly what is counted, so a new event or field changes that page too.
- `developer-brief.pdf` is printed from `_src/brief/developer-brief.html` by `node tools/brief.mjs`, which also prints the Spanish and Portuguese briefs, `es/centamont-desarrolladores.pdf` and `pt/centamont-incorporadoras.pdf`, from `_src/brief/desarrolladores.html` and `_src/brief/incorporadoras.html` (one page each, enforced). Their words follow `developers.html` and its twins; when one changes, carry the change over to all three and print again.
- Pages builds with Jekyll's defaults, which skip dot-folders, so `.claude/` is never published. Don't add a `.nojekyll` file.
- Brand: Cormorant Garamond + Jost; ink #14161A, ivory #F4F0E8, bronze #B8976A. Quiet, editorial luxury. These win over any generic suggestion from the `ui-ux-pro-max`, `design-taste-frontend` or `impeccable` skills (for example, it may propose glassmorphism or bright palettes; don't use them).
- Every piece of reader-facing text (site copy, journal notes, social posts, emails) gets the `clean-user-facing-text` pass before it ships: strip hidden Unicode, then rewrite only if its stylometry tier is `high`, keeping every fact, number and required disclosure. Dylan's standing rule (2026-10-07).
- Public-content rules: no project names or renderings without the developer's written approval; never present AI imagery as a real building or listing; keep fees, partner terms and competitor comparisons off the site.
- `.claude/skills/design-taste-frontend/` is a vendored copy of the main skill from github.com/Leonxlnx/taste-skill (MIT, commit e3c9203). The site stays plain HTML/CSS/JS; ignore its React, Tailwind and npm defaults.
- `.claude/skills/impeccable/` is a vendored copy of github.com/pbakaus/impeccable's Claude skill (Apache-2.0, commit 12cddef), copied because the cloud sandbox can't download its signed release bundle. Its design guidance and commands work; its detector engine binary isn't bundled.
- `.claude/skills/{emil-design-eng,animate,improve-animations,review-animations,find-animation-opportunities,animation-vocabulary,break-ui}/` are vendored from github.com/emilkowalski/skill (MIT, commit e8a175d). The Swift, Expo, mobile-native, Apple-design, Sonner, prototype and UI-library skills from that repo were left out as off-stack.
- `.claude/skills/ui-ux-pro-max/` is a vendored copy of github.com/nextlevelbuilder/ui-ux-pro-max-skill (MIT, commit 477bcb2). Run its scripts from the repo root.
- `.claude/skills/clean-user-facing-text/` is vendored from github.com/guillaumemeyer/watermarks-remover (MIT, commit 1181fd4), text skill only. Its `remove-ai-marks` skill and auto-strip hook were left out on purpose: they remove AI labels from images. AI renderings of unbuilt buildings stay labeled as renderings.
- `.claude/skills/{performance-optimization,shipping-and-launch}/` are vendored from github.com/addyosmani/agent-skills (MIT, commit aeac41d), with the shared checklists they cite copied into each skill's `references/`. The repo's other 23 skills and its hooks were left out: they overlap the built-in review, testing and security commands. Its backend and React advice doesn't apply to this plain static site.
- `.claude/skills/{grill-me,grilling}/` are vendored from github.com/mattpocock/skills (MIT, commit 49dd158). The rest of that repo assumes a software team with an issue tracker and was left out.
