For developers

Your building, sold
from the first sketch.

Centamont advises developers of new residential buildings, from pricing and the unit mix before design is final to the last closing. We take on few projects, and a founder stays on every one of them.

What you receive

A pricing and unit-mix study before the design is final. A launch calendar. A cooperating broker program. A project website under your name. A release plan. A weekly report against the plan. Specimens at centamont.com/developers.

Questions developers ask

Our sales team and outside brokers? On a building we advise for its developer, our advice goes to the developer alone. Outside brokers work under a program written before launch, and the report counts what they bring.

What we send you? We keep it private. Your building’s name, drawings and figures appear only with your written approval.

Do you also advise buyers? Yes, by invitation, and never on a building we advise for its developer.

How an engagement begins

A first conversation with the partners, who read every letter and reply personally. Then a study of the project: financing, approvals, design team and track record. Then a written proposal for the building, with the strategy, the team and the milestones we will report against.

An engagement in six steps

  1. We arrive while the building is still a sketch.Advisory begins before the design is final, so the building is shaped around the buyer who will live there.
  2. A strategy, and the release that sets the price.Positioning, buyer targets, campaigns and a first release that sets the price, with a founder on the account from the first meeting to the last closing.
  3. Launch day, with everything already running.Before the first reservation, the project website, search campaigns, market notes, social media and the business press are all live. A small, senior team works the launch.
  4. Half sold, and ground breaks.Once the lender’s pre-sale threshold is under contract, the construction loan can close. One weekly report shows what changes next.
  5. Topped off, and priced to the last floor.All through construction, releases and prices follow demand, so the final residences are priced as carefully as the first.
  6. The last closing is not the last of us.We stay in touch with every buyer who would like us to, so the next building begins with people who already know us.
