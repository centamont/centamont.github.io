Centamont · Markets

Every market, in the order we open.

South Florida and New York are open now. Los Angeles and Dallas are planned next, six more markets come later, and then international markets, through licensed local partners.

Open now

  1. South FloridaMiami, Fort Lauderdale and West Palm Beach on one stretch of coast.
  2. New York CityManhattan’s new condominiums, from the Hudson to the East River.
  3. The HamptonsSouthampton, Bridgehampton, East Hampton and the villages of the South Fork.

Planned

  1. Los AngelesThe Westside, from Beverly Hills and Century City to the coast, and Downtown’s towers.
  2. DallasUptown, Turtle Creek and the Arts District, where Dallas buyers trade a house for a tower.

Later

  1. TampaDowntown and Water Street, Harbour Island and Bayshore Boulevard, at the head of Tampa Bay.
  2. NaplesPelican Bay, Park Shore and the old town, on the Gulf coast.
  3. AustinRainey Street, the Second Street District and the towers along Lady Bird Lake.
  4. HoustonUptown, River Oaks and the Museum District.
  5. San FranciscoRincon Hill and the Transbay towers, Mission Bay and the city’s hills.
  6. Orange CountyNewport Center, the planned city of Irvine and the low towns of the coast.
  7. InternationalThrough licensed local partners, one market at a time.

Planning a building in one of these markets? Write to the partners.
