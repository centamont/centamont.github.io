# Product

Source: Centamont Business Plan 1.0 (Dylan, 2026-09-26), which Dylan named as the authority for this project ("Business plan 1.0 has everything you need"). Facts below come from it unless marked.

## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML/CSS/JS in one `index.html`, served by GitHub Pages at centamont.com. No build step. Playwright checks in `tests/`.

## Users

1. **Developers of new residential buildings** (Miami first), deciding who will advise on and sell a project before launch. They judge whether a firm will sell units faster, at stronger prices, with fewer surprises, and whether a senior person will actually be on their account.
2. **Private buyers at the very top of the market** looking for pre-launch and off-market residences, who expect discretion and one advisor.
3. **Top-producing agents** considering joining, by invitation only.

## Product Purpose

Centamont helps developers sell new buildings faster, with a sales approach built for each development. It advises before launch (pricing, unit mix, floor plans, amenities, buyer profile, launch strategy), runs sales and marketing, and operates a highly selective brokerage at the top end. Success for the site: a developer or qualified buyer starts a private conversation with the partners.

## Positioning

A founder on every account, a strategy written for one building rather than a template, and sales run like a data business: measured, reported weekly, improved every week. In early, before the design is final. Paid as the project succeeds. One name, built in layers to last ("Centamont": centum + mont, the hundredth blow of the stonecutter).

## Operating Context

Developers evaluate firms through pitches, case studies, weekly sales dashboards, velocity math (what each unsold month costs), and references. Buyers reach out discreetly and expect invitation-only access. Markets in order: Miami, Broward & Palm Beach, NYC & the Hamptons, then Los Angeles and Dallas, later Austin, Houston and San Francisco, then international.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Contact today is a private message to @centamont on Instagram or LinkedIn. No company email or form backend yet (open).
- No partner names or bios on the site until Dylan confirms (open).
- No brokerage license line until the Florida brokerage is registered (open).
- Static hosting only; forms need a backend before they can return.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: Centamont, one public name, no "Realty"/"Advisory" suffix. Handles: @centamont on Instagram, Threads, X, YouTube, LinkedIn, TikTok.
- Quiet luxury taken further: restraint, craftsmanship, precision; recognized without being loud. Closer to a luxury house than a property portal.
- Official logo and brand book will come from a luxury design studio; current marks are interim.

## Evidence on Hand

None public yet. No case studies, testimonials, sales figures, client names, press, project names or renderings. Case studies appear only with each developer's written approval. Never fabricate proof, and never present generated imagery as a real building or listing.

## Product Principles

1. Senior attention is the product: show it, don't claim it loudly.
2. Proof before promises: numbers and case studies only when real.
3. Discretion is a feature for buyers; clarity is a feature for developers.
4. Every public detail is held to the same standard as the business card.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Fair-housing review applies to every page and ad. WCAG AA contrast, keyboard access and reduced-motion support as a baseline (inferred standard, not separately specified).
