The neighborhood

Edgewater lies along the bay, between downtown and the Design District.

A bayfront park and promenade within the neighborhood, the galleries and restaurants of the Design District and Wynwood a short drive inland, and downtown and the causeways to Miami Beach close by.

For brokers

Brokers are welcome, and registered from the first contact.

  • Floor plans for every line, ready to send to a client.
  • The current price list and availability, sent again whenever either changes.
  • Co-op registration, so a client is recorded as yours from the first inquiry.
  • The deposit schedule and a summary of the purchase terms.
  • Approved drawings and photographs, with the terms for using them.
  • Private tours for brokers and their clients, booked with the sales gallery.

What we measure here

Each registration carries its source: search, social, a broker, a referral or the site itself. Tour requests and reservations are counted the same way, back to the source that first brought each buyer.

Every Monday those counts arrive in the weekly report beside the plan, so a developer sees which channels bring buyers who tour and reserve, not only inquiries.
