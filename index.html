Private clients

Your building, sold like it is the only one we have.

Large firms juggle dozens of projects. We take on few, and a founder stays on every one of them. Here is how a building comes to market with us beside it.

  1. We arrive while the building is still a sketch.

    Advisory begins before the design is final, so the building is shaped around the buyer who will actually live there.

    • Pricing and unit mix
    • Floor plans and amenities
    • Buyer profile and launch strategy

  2. A strategy written for this building alone.

    No template. Positioning, buyer targets and campaigns are designed around your project. A founder sits on your account from the first meeting to the final closing, with no hand-off to juniors after signing.

  3. Launch day, with everything already running.

    The project website, search, journal and market updates, social and business media are live before the first reservation.

    A small team of top brokers works the launch, each owning a buyer segment, under one set of rules and one stream of data.

  4. The numbers, every week.

    Leads, tours, reservations, contracts and pricing moves arrive in one weekly report. You always know where the building stands and what we are changing next.

  5. Topped off, and still a step ahead.

    As the structure rises, pricing and releases move with demand, so the last floors sell as well as the first.

  6. The last closing is not the last of us.

    We are paid as the project succeeds. And every buyer we meet stays with the firm, so the next building sells faster than this one.

How an engagement begins.

  1. A first conversation with the partners.Tell us about the site, the timeline and where design stands.
  2. We study the project.Financing, approvals, design team and track record. We take on few buildings, and only ones we believe in.
  3. A written proposal for your building.The strategy, the team and the milestones we will report against.
