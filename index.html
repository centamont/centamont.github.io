Private clients

Your building, sold like it is the only one we have.

We take on few projects, and a founder stays on every one of them. Here is how a building comes to market with us beside it.

  1. We arrive while the building is still a sketch.

    Advisory begins before the design is final, so the building is shaped around the buyer who will actually live there.

    • Pricing and unit mix
    • Floor plans and amenities
    • Buyer profile and launch strategy

  2. A strategy written for this building alone.

    No template. Positioning, buyer targets and campaigns are designed around your project. A founder sits on your account from the first meeting to the final closing, with no hand-off to juniors after signing.

  3. Launch day, with everything already running.

    The project website, search, journal and market updates, social and business media are live before the first reservation.

    A small team of top brokers works the launch, each owning a buyer segment, under one set of rules and one stream of data.

  4. The numbers, every week.

    Leads, tours, reservations, contracts and pricing moves arrive in one weekly report. You always know where the building stands and what we are changing next.

    See a specimen report

  5. Topped off, and still a step ahead.

    As the structure rises, pricing and releases move with demand, so the last floors sell as well as the first.

  6. The last closing is not the last of us.

    Every buyer we meet stays with the firm, so the next building sells faster than this one.

Questions, answered plainly.

How do we reach the partners?

Send a private message to @centamont on Instagram. A partner reads every one and replies personally. The letter at the foot of this page will write it for you.

When should a developer bring Centamont in?

Before the design is final. Advisory begins while the building is still a sketch: pricing and unit mix, floor plans and amenities, the buyer profile and the launch strategy, shaped around the people who will actually live there.

Who works on our building?

A founder sits on every account, from the first meeting to the final closing, with no hand-off to juniors after signing. We take on few projects, and only ones we believe in.

How will we know how sales are going?

Every Monday you receive the same one-sheet report: enquiries, tours, reservations, contracts and pricing moves, measured against the plan, with what we are changing next. See a specimen.

Do you advise buyers?

Yes, through a small, invitation-only practice for buyers at the very top of the market, with early access to new developments before they launch. Write with your market and price range. How private client advisory works.

Which markets do you work in?

Miami first, then Broward and Palm Beach, then New York and the Hamptons, followed by Los Angeles and Dallas. Austin, Houston and San Francisco come later, then international markets. We open a new market only when the last one stands on its own.

How do advisors join?

By invitation or referral. We don't post openings. Joining takes a review of verified, closed production, an interview with both partners and a live sales plan for a real project.
