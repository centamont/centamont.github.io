How it works: private client advisory

Your building, sold as if it were the only one we have.

We take on few projects, and a partner stays on every one of them. Here is how a building comes to market with us beside it.

  1. We arrive while the building is still a sketch.

    Advisory begins before the design is final, so the building is shaped around the buyer who will actually live there.

    • Pricing and unit mix
    • Floor plans and amenities
    • Buyer profile and launch strategy

    Illustration, not a real project: the site plan of a generic tower, before the design is final.

  2. A strategy, and the release that sets the price.

    Positioning, buyer targets and campaigns are written for this building alone, with a first release chosen to set the price: here, the upper floors, offered first.

    Illustration: the tower’s envelope, with the first release planned on the top floors.

  3. Launch day, with everything already running.

    Before the first reservation, the project website, search campaigns, market notes, social media and the business press are all live. Here, 18 of 62 residences are under contract within 90 days, before ground breaks.

    A small, senior sales team works the launch, each member owning a buyer segment, under one set of rules and one stream of data.

    Illustration: 18 of 62 residences under contract in the first 90 days, before construction begins.

  4. Half sold, and ground breaks.

    With half the residences under contract, the lender’s pre-sale threshold in this example, the construction loan can close and the frame rises.

    From then on, inquiries, tours, reservations, contracts and pricing moves arrive in one weekly report, with what we are changing next.

    See a specimen report

    Illustration: 32 of 62 residences under contract, past the lender’s 50 percent threshold, so ground breaks; reading on, the frame rises level by level to 34 as sales reach 46.

  5. Topped off, and priced to the last floor.

    All through construction, releases and prices follow demand, so the final residences are priced as carefully as the first ones were.

    Illustration: the structure tops off at 34 levels with the façade still closing; 46 of 62 residences under contract.

  6. The last closing is not the last of us.

    We stay in touch with every buyer who would like us to, so the next building begins with people who already know us.

    Illustration: the building is complete and all 62 residences have closed.

Sales against the schedule.

Every pricing decision moves two numbers: the month the lender’s pre-sale threshold is met, and how many residences are still unsold when the building is finished. Move the launch price and watch both.

How the curve is modeled
Pace at par
Each month, 3.5 percent of the residences still for sale go under contract.
Launch
Three times that pace in the first three months.
Ground broken
1.3 times the pace in the two months after ground breaks.
Top-off
1.5 times the pace in the three months after the building tops off.
Completion
1.25 times the pace once the building is finished.
Launch price
Every rate is multiplied by e−0.05p, where p is the price against comparable buildings in percent, from −10 to +10: about 5 percent slower for each percent above, and faster below.
Threshold
50 percent of the residences under contract, unless the slider sets it between 30 and 70 percent.
Schedule
Ground breaks in the planned month 12, or in the month the threshold is met if that comes later. Construction takes 24 months, and the building tops off 6 months before completion.

How an advisor joins

Questions, answered plainly.

The short answers; the sections above give each in full.

When should a developer bring Centamont in?

Before the design is final. Advisory begins while the building is still a sketch: pricing and unit mix, floor plans and amenities, the buyer profile and the launch strategy.

Who works on our building?

A partner leads the account from the first meeting to the last closing, and the person you meet is the person who reports to you each Monday.

More questions
How will we know how sales are going?

Every Monday you receive the same one-sheet report, measured against the plan, with what we are changing next. See a specimen.

Do you advise buyers?

Yes, through a small, invitation-only practice for private buyers, with early access to new developments before they launch, when we can. To ask for an invitation, write with your market and price range. How private client advisory works.

Whose side are you on when you advise both developers and buyers?

On a building we advise for its developer, we advise the developer alone, and we don’t advise private clients on that building.

Which markets do you work in?

Miami, Broward and Palm Beach, with New York and the Hamptons, open now. Then Los Angeles and Dallas. Tampa, Naples, Austin, Houston, San Francisco and Orange County come later, then international markets.
