# Design

The visual world of centamont.com. Product facts live in PRODUCT.md; the brand palette and fonts are fixed by Dylan ("Keep brand", 2026-10-07).

## World

An architect's elevation sheet on ivory vellum. Ink linework, bronze for what is committed, dimension lines, ground hatching and level marks as the only ornament. Every page stands on grade, and the footer is the drawing's title block.

## Tokens

- Ink #14161A, ivory #F4F0E8 (page), paper #FAF7F1, bronze #B8976A (fills, marks on ink), bronze text #7A5F3A (text on ivory), muted #4A463F, faint #6E675D, rule #D6CDBB.
- Every color in a rule comes from a token: the fixed colors by name (`--ink`, `--ivory`, the bronze steps) and, for hairlines and veils at a fraction, as channels (`--ink-rgb`, `--ivory-rgb`, `--bronze-rgb`, `--bronze-300-rgb`).
- Bronze is one hue in five named steps (`--bronze-300` #C9A877 night accent, `-500` #B8976A fill, `-600` #9C7C50 line on ivory, `-700` #7A5F3A text on ivory, `-800` #554836 edge at night). Patina is the rare second color, the grey-green bronze turns after a century outdoors (`--patina`: #4B685C by day, 5.4:1 on ivory; #8AA69B at night, 6.4:1 on the page; each step at the OKLCH lightness of its bronze partner, -700 by day and -500 at night, so it reads as green at text size and weighs the same as the bronze beside it): it marks markets open now (the home map and list, a point beside each open market in the Markets menu, named for screen readers as "open now", the "Opening now" status on the market pages) and the journal's note numbers, 3 to 5 percent of any surface, never buttons, links or prices (Dylan, 2026-10-17).
- Dark scheme: page #1B1E23, paper #21252A, text #EDE7DC, muted #C2BBAF (8.8:1 on the page), faint #9A9388 (5.5:1 on the page, 5.1:1 on paper), rule #2F3236, grid #45494F, accent #C9A877, linework turns ivory. The ink grounds (hero, private door, name, the intro's curtain, the header over them, the page change's ground and the browser's bar over them) are the brand ink taken deeper, #090A0C in the same cool hue as #14161A (`--hero-rgb` 9,10,12; band rule #2A2D31), framed in .42 bronze hairlines: one temperature with the graphite page, a step down of 1.185:1 from it (1.285:1 from paper), so they still read as rooms at night. On the ink, text keeps AA and more: ivory 17.4:1, band text #CFC8BA 11.9:1, the hero lede 13.2:1, night bronze 8.8:1.
- Cormorant Garamond 300 for display, 400 for h3 and large reading text; Jost 400 at 18px for body (muted #4A463F), 500 for buttons and small uppercase labels. Nothing below 15px, except small-caps labels and drawing annotations at 13px.
- Reading floors: running and reading text is 16px or more (body and the answers to the questions 18px; links, notes and disclaimers 16px); captions keep 15px; uppercase labels are 14px Jost 500 at .12 to .16em where a reader reads them (the menu, buttons, meta lines, keys, a market's status, the letter's labels: `--t-nav`, `--t-label`), and 13px only inside drawings and dense instruments, never smaller. A label, key or legend that carries information (the tower's gauge, the instrument's sliders, readouts and key, the deposit scale, a market's status, a note's kind) is set in muted, AAA on ivory; faint is kept for incidental metadata (axis ticks, ordinals, a keyboard hint) and captions.
- Type ladder (tokens in `:root`, one size per role; a heading is never smaller than one below it in rank, and nothing but the close outranks the h1). Values at 1440x900, then 390:
  - `--t-display` 136 / 48: the close, "Write to the partners."
  - `--t-hero` 112 / 48: the one h1 (smaller on short windows, so the buttons stay above the fold; there the spreads can stand larger than it).
  - `--t-spread` 104 / 42: the full-measure spreads, the markets h2 and the name.
  - `--t-chapter` 81 / 39: the chapter openers (services, developers) and every subpage's h1 except the report's.
  - `--t-section` 56 / 39: the back-matter heads (journal, people, questions) and the private door. On phones every section head shares the chapter size, except the markets spread and the close.
  - `--t-head` 40 / 29: every h3, and a note's h2.
  - `--t-lede` 32 / 24: the lead lines that answer a heading across a spread (the two ink bands, the advisors).
  - `--t-big` 26 / 21: other lead lines, the questions, standfirsts.
  - `--t-num` 109 / 80: the developer story's step numerals, the chapter's drawing-sheet signature (on a phone held upright they stand on their own line just over their heading, set on a close .7 line, and grow with the screen's height: 36px on a short screen, 42 at 664 tall, 71 at 844, about two and a half times the heading, and 72 from about 850; a phone on its side sets them at about twice the heading, 44 to 60px; the steps not in view keep theirs at 40%).
  Tablets (521 to 820px) lift chapter and spread to keep their rank over the back matter. Past 1440px the heads and lead lines keep opening up a little (the close 155, the chapters 94 and an h3 46 at 1920), as the column does from 1600px; the h1 holds at 116, so the hero tower keeps its frame, and the spreads stay under it; the reading pages' titles (the notes, privacy, the 404) hold at 81, since their 880px column does not widen.

## Drawing conventions

- Pens (`--lw-*`): profile 1.5px for ground lines, outer silhouettes, the table top and the deposit bar's frame; object 1px for building edges, the outer arch, the ruler's base and chart frames; detail 0.6px for inner bays, door leaves, the inner arch and the ruler's minor ticks; hatch 0.6px at 50% opacity, at 45 degrees. Strokes keep their screen width however a drawing scales (`vector-effect: non-scaling-stroke`, or the facade's `--fu`).
- Line types (`--lt-*`): continuous for what exists or is open now; hidden (4 3) for what is planned or projected; dot (1 3) for what comes later or is for reference; datum (12 3 2 3) for datums, boundaries and thresholds.
- By day a bronze that is a line, a point, a meter or a hatch takes the line bronze (`--sold-line`, 3.6:1 on paper); the fill bronze is for areas and bars. At night and in the ink rooms the fill bronze serves both.
- Bronze, in a drawing, marks only what is committed: levels sold, deposits held in escrow. Markets open now take patina, so open never reads as sold. What is possible or planned stays ink, so the advisors' table carries no bronze.
- One caption strip: a 1px rule, a 10px gap, the sentence on the left in Jost 15px faint (disclosures word for word, never below 15px), and on the right only live information or a key, in Jost 500 13px, .14em, uppercase. No figure numbers. The facade, the doorways and the ruler stay uncaptioned.
- Figures sit on the sheet between four 14px corner ticks (`.ticks`) in the faint tone, never in a box, so the ticks never outweigh what they frame; no label inside comes within 8px of a tick.
- A document laid on the page (the specimen report, and its card on the home page) is a sheet of paper, not a figure: it keeps its 1px edge, with its corners marked in an ink tone (the card's edges run on 8px past each corner in its ink, the report's corners take faint L marks), never bronze; under the card the sheet's own edge stands for the caption strip's rule.
- Ground line: the footer's 1px ink rule over a 9px band of 45-degree hatch at 14px pitch, inside the page width.
- Title block: Practice, Office, Drawn, Scale and Sheet over Work with us, Sheets and Follow. The sheet number is its largest figure (72px; 40px on phones), set under its label like every other value, then a rule and the sheet's name on one line (where the cell is too narrow, the size of the set is left off rather than wrap). Drawn comes only from a page's own date metadata, otherwise October 2026; Scale reads Not to scale (the report: Specimen); Sheets lists 01 to 06 with the current sheet in ink and underlined.

## Composition

- One scroll page with anchored sections (old hash URLs land on their section). One h1 in the hero; every section opens with an h2 and its intro directly beneath it. Order: hero, three practices, developer story and how an engagement begins, private clients, markets, notes, people, the name, contact.
- Three ink grounds: the hero, private clients (the private door) and the name. The footer opens with a full-width CENTAMONT signature. Everything else sits on ivory between 1px rules.
- Section heads share the width: the h2 on the left seven of twelve columns, its intro on the right five, opened by a short bronze rule, the two on one last baseline. The two chapter openers (services, developers) set a full-measure h2 on two lines and step the intro down into the right five; markets runs its h2 full-measure at spread scale; the advisors keep a short head on the home page (the h2, its intro, and a link to the Advisors page under the intro in the right five), and the Advisors page sets the seat plan across the full measure, the lead line on the left half under it and the steps and the invitation on the right five; the journal opens as a nameplate, its h2 on one line on the page's axis with the intro centred under it and a double rule where the feature begins; the questions read as a ledger across the full measure, each row numbered 01 to 05 in the display face in the margin, the question, its answer, the head and the closing line all on one indent. In the name band the lead line stands on the left under the ruler's first half, the paragraph on the right under its second.
- A padding score: chapter openers (services, developers, markets) open loud, clamp(88px,9.6vw,136px) above; the back matter (journal, people, questions) reads quiet, clamp(56px,5.6vw,84px) above and below; the ink bands and the close keep their own.
- Rhythm: between the two ink bands one section is a set piece, not the head module repeated. The journal sets its guide as one article: the title, its standfirst in the display face and its meta beside the deposit schedule, which is a plate inside the measure, framed like every figure (corner ticks, then the caption strip), with the price marked off above the bar in the display face like a dimension string (two steps under the guide's title, in muted, so it never outweighs the title or the key; the bar's own end stands for zero) and the key in ruled rows under it, each key in bold, then what it means. The guide's links follow its meta line, at the text's own spacing, beside the plate; on a phone, where the plate follows the meta line at once, they close the article just under the caption. No second set piece joins it there.
- The phone hero gives the tower the first half of the screen: the headline starts at clamp(46svh, 100svh - 416px, 56svh), the lede sets at 17px on a 1.5 line and the buttons at 14px padding, so both buttons still stand above the fold of a 360x780 screen and up, and the drawing fits between the survey note (14px under it) and 28px above the headline; at 390x844 the tower stands about a quarter taller than before.
- The developer story keeps one pace beside the tower: every step is as tall as its words, one measured gap apart. The last step's words arrive at that gap and stop at the foot of the sheet, their last line on the baseline of the drawing's caption, beside the finished building through its runway (beside the tower the step is 72svh tall, enough for the last closing view to settle, and the numerals stand at 7.6vw, so the story runs about 3,170px at 1440x900 rather than 3,630); then words and drawing leave together, line for line, so no fragment of the words is left beside the drawing and the drawing never stands beside an empty column. Beside the tower a step turns as one: the step the drawing shows stands in ink (bronze numeral, ink heading, muted words), and the steps before and after it stand back together (a steady ghost numeral, words in faint, still AA), so two steps never compete in one window; on a phone held upright, where the steps pass one at a time under the drawing, the words keep their ink, and a phone on its side, which sets the steps beside the drawing, turns them as one like a wider screen. Step 05 keeps the drawing until its first line meets the bar, so it reads whole and alone once 04 has passed under the bar: the hand-over to 06 moves down the gap between them, which stays the same measured gap.
- Every section carries one drawing that holds real information: the practice marks, the tower and its live level gauge, the doorway framing the private-client terms, a surveyed markets map (simplified coastline, true coordinates, labels with halos) with a South Florida detail and a world key, the deposit schedule, the advisors' table drawn as a plan of seats (ten in ink for launch, ten more in hidden line, two dimension strings) on its own Advisors page, the hundred-blow ruler, and an empty lot on grade on the 404. The colophon shows the pens, line types and marks at 1:1.
- The page closes at display scale: "Write to the partners.", its intro with the @centamont handle across from it, then the letter, with the handle again as one quiet line beside its button.
- On phones the drawings are composed for the hand, not folded from the desktop. The developer story's sticky panel gives the canvas the height (46svh + 38px for the whole figure) and reads as a drawing sheet: the caption strip on one line above the drawing, the gauge under it as a title block in three bands with air between them (Levels framed and Under contract, each label over its figure; then the meter under the residences, its 50% tick a small cross on the line, 10px clear of the figures; then the tick's label, ending under the tick, 8px clear of it, and a 10px foot of the panel's ground under it before a long fade; a panel too narrow for both counters takes each on a line and centres the label on the tick). The drawing is masked off a few pixels short of the caption and of the gauge's rule, so no linework runs up to either. A phone on its side keeps the same sheet beside the steps, and the instrument's heading holds under the header beside its chart, on an opaque band of its own ground with a long fade under it while it holds, so the words pass under it dissolved, never cut. The step headings take the full measure and break in two lines (three for the first on the narrowest phones; "The numbers, / every week." keeps its break). Where the practices stack (a tablet and a phone) the facade stays, inside the gutters, its profile lines at the hairline: one building of three bays as the index of the three practices, each bay lettered I, II, III under its ground, and each practice carrying its numeral over its heading as a quiet label (the display face at 20px, in muted, not bronze); a practice in hand lights its bay and its letter. On the sales curve, where Ground broken and Top-off stack (a narrow tablet and a phone on its side), their rows stand a clear line apart; under a phone's drawing the three milestones stand one to a line, their months in a column of their own.
- Photographs (October 16): one grade over every photograph and film (saturate .84, sepia .06, contrast 1.03; a little darker at night), so the set reads as one. Full-bleed views: the home film between the practices and the developer story opens from the column's width to the window's as it arrives; each market page opens on its view, full bleed under the title, with the elevation drawing after it; a note's opening plate stands at the page's full measure, wider than its text. The markets pair is a wide view of Miami (16:10, seven columns) against a tall one of Manhattan (4:5, five columns, set lower). Captions keep the column.
- Heads vary: the practices' statement steps its intro down on the left, where the practices begin; the developer story's on the right, where its steps hang; the name band sets the hundredth blow as a pull quote across ten columns with its paragraph answering beneath on the right. The markets and the questions close with one line to the partners in the display face (over a rule at the markets; the questions' last row is its rule); the hero carries a quiet "Or write to the partners" beside its buttons on wide screens.
- Lists are ruled rows, never card grids. No eyebrows above headings; meta lines go below the title.
- The masthead: the lockup, then five sections (Developers, Private clients, Markets, Journal, Contact) set in the bar from 1280px; Markets is a word with a 9px chevron that opens a ruled list of the markets hanging from the header's foot (Florida, New York, California, then Texas as a quiet label over Dallas, Austin and Houston, set in from its edge), on a click, a key (Enter, Arrow Down, Escape) or a resting mouse, and in the drawer the four stand as rows among the others, Texas as one row with its three cities beside it in the bar's capitals, so the drawer keeps its ten rows. The market a page belongs to, or the home page's markets section while it is read, gives Markets the dimension mark. In the bar the motion switch carries its word, Motion, beside its glyph, the current one standing on a short bronze dimension mark; What we do and Advisors live in the drawer and the footer, and the footer's Sheets list runs 01 to 07, in two columns of four and three where its cell is wide enough. Pause and Night are 18px glyphs without a box, 44px to the touch, parted by a 1px by 16px rule, each named for screen readers and titled ("Pause motion", "Night mode"). Below 1024px, or when larger text would crowd the bar, the sections fold into the drawer, which opens along a 1px rule riding its edge and closes on @centamont, in Cormorant at 26px, beside the motion switch on phones. On phones (to 820px) the header steps aside as the reader reads on down (more than 8px) and comes back at the first move up, at the top, with the menu open, on a jump and when focus comes into it (with motion reduced or paused it stays); `--hdr` (64px, or 0 while it is away) sets what stands under it.
- The browser's own bar takes the ground under the header: the one theme-color reads the ink of the hero or a band, or the page, and follows day and night.
- A faint vellum grain (grain.png) sits over every ground.

## Phone page budget

The home page at 390x844, section by section (R7p, October 2026): hero 905, practices 1,475, developers 5,650 (of which the story 2,530), private clients 1,267, markets 1,924, journal 1,398, advisors 1,352, questions 878, the name 795, contact 1,226, footer 768: 17,638px, 20.9 screens (from 18,181, 21.5, before the phone composition rounds). The goal is 16 screens (13,504px). Spacing alone cannot reach it: the whole page holds about 3,400px of vertical white space, less than the 4,100px still to find, so the rest would have to come out of figures or copy, which the goal does not allow. Anything added (a case study, portraits) is measured at 390x844 and comes in against these figures, paid for in its own section where it can be.

## Motion

The signature is a line-drawn 3D massing model (`model.js`, canvas 2D, no libraries) of a generic tower, always captioned as an illustration. It appears twice: in the ink hero, rising floor by floor after the intro and orbiting slowly, with a slight tilt that follows the pointer; and in the developer story, where it frames and fills bronze per step while the camera moves between views.
Supporting moments: a one-time intro per visitor, remembered on the device (a counter from 001 to 100, counted from the first paint; on the hundredth a bronze hairline cracks the numeral and the two slabs part, each carrying its half of it on a bronze cut face, and the halves of the numeral fade before the headline rises where they stood; the page is open in about a second and a half; any click, key or scroll skips it); the hero headline rising line by line in the open while the slabs part; the doorway drawing itself around the private-client terms; market routes drawing out from South Florida on a latitude and longitude chart; the name cut in line by line, and its band's foot cracking along a bronze hairline, then parting into a peak at the mark's pitch with straight flanks, the crack left as one bronze vein up its middle, as the ruler's hundredth tick flashes once.
Headlines rise only at the opening, the place and the close: the hero h1, the markets h2, the contact h2, and each subpage's h1. The lede under a heading never waits for it: it stands from the start, everywhere. Every other heading stands still; a section's intro only draws its bronze rule as the section arrives. Ink grounds arrive full bleed and still: the doorway and the ruler are their only actors. The header takes the ink grounds without a tween. The hero's ground passes through the bar in step with the page: the ink is cut to the part of the bar over it, and the type turns as its foot crosses the bar's middle. Each band takes the bar whole, at once, as it reaches the bar's foot, and gives it back as its own foot passes, so a jump to the private door lands under its ink and a jump to the place after a band lands under ivory.
A same-page jump of more than one and a half screens never flies through the drawings: it passes under a veil of the page's own grounds (180 ms up, 260 ms away), or happens under the open menu before the menu draws up; the heading it lands on takes the focus. Shorter jumps scroll. Between pages the root fades through (160 ms out, 360 ms in) over a ground, with the header held.
One motion system, in site.css: --ease-out for what arrives or answers, --ease-ink for lines that draw, --ease-display for display type, --ease-drawer for the menu, and four durations (--t-press 120 ms, --t-ui 220 ms, --t-move 360 ms, --t-ink 1 s; a drawing starts as its top clears the lowest tenth of the screen, so it is finished by the time it stands mid-screen). No expo tail on a stroke; an exit takes at most 70% of its entrance.
Reduced motion and Pause motion: no intro, no orbit, final states shown at once, jumps instant; Pause reloads the page where the reader was. Day and night toggle in the header, remembered per visitor; the turn is a transition of its own (never the page-to-page fade): the new theme is uncovered from the header down over the old one held still (360 ms, --ease-ink), so the two are never mixed and no frame passes through grey, the drawings recoloured inside it and the sun and moon trading places with a 45 degree turn over 200 ms; instant with motion reduced or paused.

## Browser surfaces

Hover answers only a fine pointer that can hover (`(hover:hover) and (pointer:fine)`); focus rings answer everywhere. The browser's tap flash is off everywhere, and a touch answers in the next frame and lets go (site.js marks the control as the finger lands, where the browser's own :active waits about 150 ms): a button fills under the finger in 120 ms, a ruled row draws its ink rule and arrow, a link takes the ink, Menu and the switches take an ink edge, and nothing stays lit after the finger lifts or the touch turns into a scroll. Bronze never answers a hover or a press: text answers in ink, rows with their rule and arrow; what is bronze at rest stays bronze.
Links hand their underline over: the old line slips away to the right (220 ms) while, 140 ms in, a new one draws from the left (260 ms), so the line is never gone. Buttons fill from the left as a copy of the label in the fill's colours is uncovered over it (380 ms in, 260 ms out, --ease-out, so the fill moves the moment the pointer arrives), so no letter ever turns grey or ink on ink; the arrow steps 4px. Ruled rows that lead somewhere answer with the rule: a 1px ink line draws over the row's top rule from the left (300 ms, --ease-out) and an 18 by 8 arrow draws in at its end in step with it, both done together; the title keeps its ink. A question, and a note's next-note link, answer the same way along their foot rule. Choosing a voice in the letter, the bronze dimension line travels to the chosen label (280 ms), the old paragraph goes (120 ms) and the sheet takes the new one's height (240 ms) as it settles in. Lining, tabular numerals wherever a number counts.

Bronze selection, bronze caret, 1px bronze focus ring at 5px offset, rule-colored scrollbar, tabular numerals in the title block.

Safe areas: the copy, the header bar included, keeps the gutter or the device's safe-area inset, whichever is wider (`--gl`, `--gr`); grounds and the figures that bleed to the edge still run to it.
