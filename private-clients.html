Private clients · How it works

A short list, made for one buyer.

Private client advisory begins with a brief and ends with the keys. One advisor carries it the whole way, and a partner reads every first message.

How a brief works

  1. A private message. Write to @centamont on Instagram with the market you have in mind and a price range. A partner replies personally.
  2. A conversation, not a form. How you will live in the residence, when you hope to move, and what you will not compromise on.
  3. A short list. A few residences chosen for your brief, including new developments shown before their public launch when we can.
  4. One advisor to the end. The same person from the first viewing to the closing table, and after it.

Discretion, in writing

Your name stays with us. We never publish who we advise, and nothing you tell us appears in our marketing.

Viewings are private. Residences on your list are shown by appointment, never advertised under your brief.

What you share stays with your brief. We use it only to work for you, as our privacy page sets out.

Tell us the market and the range. We will tell you, honestly, whether we are the right advisors for it.
